## 2025 Competitions

### CyberQ Hackathon 2025
**Website**: https://cyberq.ctf.ae/  
**Dates**: October 20-21, 2025 (Online Qualifiers) | November 26-27, 2025 (Finals)  
**Location**: Online + ADNEC, Abu Dhabi (Finals)  
**Prize Pool**: AED 100,000  
**Organizers**: Technology Innovation Institute (TII) & Cyber Security Council (CSC)

**Focus Areas**:
- Cybersecurity
- Post-Quantum Cryptography
- Cryptanalysis
- AI Security & Vulnerabilities
- Advanced Cryptographic Challenges

The CyberQ Hackathon is the ultimate challenge in cybersecurity, featuring next-gen cryptography, post-quantum algorithms, and AI vulnerabilities.
