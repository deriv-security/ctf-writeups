# Dubai Police CTF 2025

![Dubai Police CTF](https://dp.ctf.ae/logos/DPCTF%20LOGO/DP-CTF-logo.svg)

## Competition Overview

Dubai Police launched the 8th version of its Capture the Flag competition (DPCTF) in October 2025. The competition featured a unique smart-city hacking challenge with three distinct tracks designed for different skill levels.

**Website:** https://dp.ctf.ae/

## Competition Details

- **Total Prize Pool:** AED 223,000
- **Format:** Online Qualification + Onsite Finals
- **Location:** Dubai Police Officers Club, Dubai, UAE
- **Dates:**
  - Registration: October 1-18, 2025
  - Qualification Round: October 5-18, 2025 (Online)
  - Selection Period: October 18-21, 2025
  - Finals: October 25-26, 2025 (Onsite)

## Competition Tracks

### 1. Schools Track
- **Target Audience:** High School Students
- **Difficulty:** Beginner-friendly
- **Focus Areas:**
  - Cybersecurity fundamentals
  - Educational mentorship
  - Cyber security essentials
  - Coding exercises

### 2. Universities Track
- **Target Audience:** University Students
- **Difficulty:** Intermediate
- **Focus Areas:**
  - Practical skills
  - Attack simulations
  - Defense techniques
  - Real-world scenarios

### 3. Professionals Track
- **Target Audience:** Cybersecurity Experts
- **Difficulty:** Expert level
- **Focus Areas:**
  - Advanced exploitation
  - Web Application Security
  - Digital Forensics
  - Elite-level challenges

## Competition Format

### Qualification Round
- Online challenges across multiple categories
- Individual participation only (no teams)
- Solving more challenges increased chances of finals selection
- Open to UAE residents of all nationalities (Emirates ID required)

### Finals
- 2-day onsite competition
- Live smart-city model hacking
- 10:00 AM - 4:00 PM each day
- Selected participants only

## Key Rules

1. Individual participation only - no teams allowed
2. No sharing of solutions, hints, or answers
3. One track per participant (no switching after registration)
4. AI tools and automated assistance were permitted
5. Professional conduct and respect required throughout
